# MODEX Brand Guidelines

Follow these rules for every MODEX design or video. Check the final work against the checklist at the end before delivering.

## 1. Category colours (most important rule)
Each category has its own colour. Never mix them up.

| Category | Colour | Social/ads hex | Packaging hex (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kitchen (food prep, cooking & baking, drinks, air fryers, pressure cookers, grills, egg boilers, water dispensers) | Green | #62A737 | #6BC04B |
| Home (heaters, fans, air coolers, irons, vacuum cleaners, pressure washers, steam cleaners, carpet washers, spot cleaners, garment steamers, steam generators) | Turquoise | #23978F | #00CBBB |
| Women / personal care | Purple | #895DBF | Core panel #74349C, lilac bars/tabs #BF8BCA (copper #BC6D4F panel seen only on some women premium boxes, e.g. IPL1100) |
| Men's grooming | Blue | #3A7EBA | Core panel navy #1B3A5E, bars/tabs #3A87CF |

- Turquoise is ONLY for home — never for kitchen. Water dispensers are KITCHEN (green).
- Shared: background #1A1A1A, text white #F6F6F6, packaging charcoal #2D292A/#2E2A2B, info-panel grey #D3D2D2.
- Big store-wide events may use RED highlight: #EE1B1B (front), #BE1111 (middle), #971717 (back).
- Logo: 95% black / Pantone 10105C metallic grey, keep clear space. Use the real MODEX logo, never retype it. ALWAYS use the logo WITHOUT the ® mark. Logo PNGs (white + black, no ®): D:\MODEX\MODEX REFRESHMENT WITH 1HQ\LOGO\
- Logo may be white on dark, black on light, or SILVER (Pantone 10105C, screen #A7A8AA) on social media and the heater stand. Never flip, stretch or recolour it.
- Give the logo breathing space — never tight against the headline.
- The 1HQ agency deck colours are NOT accurate — always use the table above.

## 3. Standard layer structure
1. Background: lifestyle photo/video of the SAME product from the MODEX library.
2. Diagonal: the key brand element — ONE organized layer, running top-right corner to bottom-left corner (English). Either a dark semi-transparent panel or a category-colour gradient. Never stack many semi-transparent black layers. (Arabic versions mirror it — see section 10.)
3. One cut-out product layer on top (used for 3D-style animation: float, turn, zoom, light sweep).
4. Text: offer (white) + category line (category colour), MODEX logo, CTA "modex.store".

Cut-out variant (no lifestyle): #1A1A1A background → subtle moving texture ~37% opacity → category-colour glow behind the product → headline → product overlapping the bottom of "FRIDAY" → offer text.

## 4. Angles & shapes
- Angles come from the MODEX "X". When used with imagery, angles ALWAYS point towards the product or the person.
- Prefer triangles over circles for graphic shapes in motion design.
- Text must sit fully on one side of the diagonal — never half inside, half outside, never touching the line.

## 5. Clarity (tone: "quiet confidence, precision without noise")
- Focus on the main message; remove extra elements. The customer must understand the offer instantly.
- Text must stay readable: no heavy neon/glow on text.
- One product = hero. Product must contrast with the background.
- Spread content out — never crowded in the middle of the screen.

## 6. Layout recipes
- Single product: one large cut-out at a dynamic angle overlapping the bottom of "FRIDAY", category glow behind, logo top, "XX% OFF" + "all <category>" + small end date.
- Collection: 3 products of the same category side by side (not overlapping in a cluster), each at its best angle; hero biggest.
- Lifestyle single: product over its room photo + dark diagonal + spinning "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" ring text in the category colour, product in front of the ring.
- All-category poster: dark background with a soft multi-colour glow mixing the category colours + thin white light-beam lines; huge white "BLACK FRIDAY"; one hero product per category grouped in the middle; "UP TO XX% OFF"; modex.store + validity dates.
- Big event (red): layered red speech bubble "big savings on everything" with mixed-category cut-outs.
- Pre-promo / teaser (before sale): no %; "black friday deals" opener; one screen per category with a category-colour speech bubble "big savings on <category>"; end "don't miss out! / starts <date>"; optional days/hours/minutes countdown.
- In-store digital screens: countdown near the bottom, MODEX logo at the BOTTOM.
- End card: "DON'T MISS OUT!", end date, modex.store, (cash on delivery for Iraq).
- Starburst badge in category colour for "PLUS FREE GIFT*" with small-print condition.
- BF 2026 4:5 carousel (approved 2026-09-25): cover (giant category-colour % + product line-up) → one slide per product → free-gifts slide → end card. Two A/B versions: CUT-OUT (product on #1A1A1A with category glow, name/type/old+new price centred) and LIFESTYLE (full-bleed lifestyle photo on top + dark #1A1A1A band at the bottom with one diagonal top edge, right side higher, thin category-colour line on the edge; name + type right-aligned in the band, struck old price + big new price left, dots + SWIPE bottom-right).
  - Dark/moody photos: white logo, category-colour "BLACK FRIDAY · 15% OFF", dark top gradient. Bright photos: black logo, bold dark green headline, soft white top gradient.
  - No lifestyle photo: light studio background + same dark band; show the colour variant that contrasts, the other colour as a small "also in" swatch — no side-by-side colour duplicates.
- Arabic line (e.g. "بلاك فرايدي") may be added under the category line for bilingual English versions — set in ITC Handel Gothic Arabic. Full Arabic versions follow section 10.

## 7. Imagery rules
- Studio shows the product; lifestyle shows the benefit. Show the outcome (hot food, warm home, clean floors).
- Three-quarter view is the default hero angle, eye level, longer lenses (no wide/warped distortion).
- People are incidental: hands, movement, figures out of focus. Avoid faces, posing, looking at camera.
- Believable light source, no harsh reflections, not overly AI/synthetic-looking.
- Product fidelity: never let AI redraw or change the product's shape. Use the exact product photo/cut-out from the library (D:\MODEX\MODEX WEBSITE SHOPIFY\<MODEL>\2000X3000\ transparent PNGs; 9:16 videos in ...\9 to 16 video folders\<MODEL>\).
- Always check the MODEX logo on the product is correct (not distorted/AI) before use.
- Core tier = bright, daylight, lived-in. Premium tier = deeper tones, richer materials, controlled contrast.
- Gallery order: Recognise it → Understand it → Want it → Live with it.

## 8. Packaging (product box / gift box) — Core vs Premium (checked 2026-10-03 on 9 real boxes)
Every category has a Core box and a Premium box. Same layout, different mood:

| | Core | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Box colour | White / light | Dark charcoal #2D292A |
| Front scene | Bright daylight lifestyle room (white kitchen, light wood, soft window light) | Dark moody lifestyle room (lamp light, deep tones) |
| Info panel | Dark category colour (kitchen dark green, women #74349C, men navy #1B3A5E) | Light grey #D3D2D2 |
| Tab + "XX FEATURES" bar | Lighter tint of the category colour | Category colour |
| 3YRS warranty seal | Category-colour seal | Grey/white seal |
| Extra | — | "PREMIUM EDITION" tab top-right |
| Logo | Black MODEX | Metallic silver/white MODEX |

Shared on both tiers:
- MODEX logo top-left; category tab under it: "KITCHEN | APPLIANCES", "HOME | APPLIANCES", or "PERSONAL | APPLIANCES" (women AND men both say PERSONAL — only the colour changes).
- Angled info panel: lowercase product name + sub-name (e.g. "air fryer / 3 litre", "vacuum cleaner / powerful upright"), "MODEL | wattage", 3 key-feature icons, "XX FEATURES" bar.
- Thin category-colour diagonal line(s) pointing to the product.
- Front: product hero in its room; personal care fronts add a small lifestyle photo (hair, foot, beard) in the panel.
- Side panel: full feature icon grid; colour options swatches when the model comes in several colours.
- Back: studio product shot with callout circles ("key product features") — Core uses a big category-colour gradient block, Premium a dark studio background.
- Top flap: MODEX + tab + "MODERN EXCELLENCE" + www.modexworld.com. Footer: "made by MODEX UK Ltd" + UK flag; barcode panel with model number.
- Product badge (on the product): silk-screen printed, white or black, MODEX + wattage/voltage + series name + 3 icons.

Reference boxes: Kitchen Core AF7700 / WD7050, Kitchen Premium AF9750 / BL940; Home Premium CHR1120 / UVC1520; Women Core HD1150 / EP1800, Women Premium CR2000 / IPL1100; Men Core HT1650, Men Premium HT1690.

## 9. 9:16 safe zone & approved winter video template (locked 2026-09-26)
- 9:16 (1080x1920) safe zone: keep key info out of the top 14% (y<269), the bottom 35% (y>1248) and the right ~15% (x>918, TikTok buttons). Only the end date, small print and modex.store may sit lower.
- Winter Sale template (18s): (1) product video opener with logo + "winter sale"; (2) plain #1A1A1A cut-out card: logo, "winter" (category colour) / "sale" (white), "15% / OFF", "all <category>", PLUS FREE GIFT* starburst, product cut-out with category glow + soft floor shadow, end date and small print below the safe zone; (3) product video with model, type, struck old price, new price in IQD; (4) Rebuy-style gift-tier end card: progress bar 250,000 free delivery → 350,000 + grill → 650,000 + grill & egg boiler, gift icons kept smallish, "your cart" counter, end date + modex.store at the bottom.
- Same layout for every category — only colour, product, videos, price and category line change. Wide products (e.g. air fryers) are sized by width so they look as big as tall ones.

## 10. Arabic versions (locked 2026-09-26)
Full right-to-left layout, like international brands:
- All Arabic text right-aligned, starting top-right (right edge still clear of the TikTok buttons).
- MIRROR the diagonal for Arabic: dark triangle top-right, edge running top-left → bottom-right, so the Arabic text sits fully inside it. Product moves to the other side; the angle still points to the product.
- Progress bars / sequences run right-to-left (first tier on the right); icons with a direction (delivery truck) face the flow. Arabic label under each tier.
- Text blocks sit fully on one side of the diagonal — headline/logo inside the dark area; cart line, modex.store and end date fully on the lighter side.
- NEVER flip product photos/videos or the MODEX logo. Numbers, prices, model codes and modex.store stay in Latin characters.
- Iraqi copy: prices in د.ع, months as كانون الأول etc. Winter copy used: تخفيضات الشتاء · 15% خصم · على جميع المدافئ / على جميع أجهزة المطبخ · مع هدية مجانية* · ينتهي العرض 31 كانون الأول · *هدية مجانية للطلبات فوق 350,000 د.ع · هدايا الشتاء · كلما كبرت سلتك زادت هداياك · توصيل مجاني / + شواية مجانية / + شواية وسلاقة بيض · سلتك:
